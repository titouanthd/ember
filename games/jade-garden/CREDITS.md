## Poem

《春晓》 (*Spring Dawn*) by **Meng Haoran** (唐, 689–740). Public
domain.

春眠不觉晓
处处闻啼鸟
夜来风雨声

## Inspirations

- **Bejeweled** (PopCap, 2001) — match-3 core, cascade scoring.
- **Puzzle & Dragons** (GungHo, 2012) — East Asian aesthetic.
- **Monument Valley** (ustwo, 2014) — contemplative pacing.
- **Journey** (thatgamecompany, 2012) — environmental narration,
  no dialogue.
- **Zachtronics** puzzle games — shape-matched hint ring pattern.

## Engine

Built with [macroquad](https://macroquad.rs/) and the workspace-local
crates `ember-core` and `ember-stdlib`. See `Cargo.toml` for the full
dependency list.

## Sound effects

The Halloween teaser falls back to macOS built-in system sounds
(`Glass.aiff`, `Sosumi.aiff`) when no override is provided. Drop your
own `assets/scare.wav` or `assets/scream.wav` to replace them.
